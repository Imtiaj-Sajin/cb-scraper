============================================================
  CRUNCHBASE SCRAPER  -  How to use
============================================================

REQUIREMENT
-----------
Google Chrome must be installed on this PC. That is the only
requirement - nothing else to install.


HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Double-click   CrunchbaseScraper.exe

2. A black window opens and stays open. THAT WINDOW IS THE
   PROGRAM - do not close it while you are working.

3. Your web browser opens automatically at:
        http://127.0.0.1:5000
   (If it does not open, type that address into your browser.)

4. Paste the domains you were assigned (one per line), or
   upload your .txt / .csv file.

5. Click "Start scraping".

6. A Chrome window opens and clears the security check by
   itself. Leave it alone - do not close it.

7. Watch the progress table. When finished, click
   "Companies CSV" / "People CSV" to download your results.

8. To stop the program, close the black window.


LOGIN  (for funding totals - NEW in v2)
---------------------------------------
Funding total is only shown to logged-in Crunchbase users.
To collect it:

1. Tick "Use a logged-in session".
2. Either type the account email + password, then click
   "Log in now", OR leave them blank and click "Log in now"
   and sign in by hand in the Chrome window that opens.
3. Once the log says you are logged in, click "Start scraping"
   as usual. The session is remembered for next time.

(Leave "Diagnostic mode" OFF for normal work.)


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- First launch can take 10-20 seconds. Be patient.
- Windows SmartScreen / antivirus may warn about an unknown
  app. Choose "More info" -> "Run anyway" (or "Keep").
- Work in batches - do not paste thousands of domains at
  once. Crunchbase limits how fast anyone can pull data.
- If many domains start failing, wait a few minutes and
  resume; the program also pauses and recovers on its own.
- A ".browser_profile" folder will appear next to the .exe
  on first run. That is normal - it is the program's working
  data. Leave it.
